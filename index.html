How It Works

Twelve teams, one for every birth month. Each team enters the stage in two categories — a solo artist and a group/choir — and the audience decides who takes the crown, one dollar-vote at a time.

The Teams

  • Your birth month is your team. Team up with everyone born in the same month.
  • Each month fields one act — either a solo artist or a group/choir (2 or more singers). Choose wisely!
  • Songs are claimed first-come, first-served from the official 50-song playlist below.

The Rounds

  • Round 1 — Preliminaries: every month's act performs once.
  • Round 2 — Semifinals: the top 6 advance with a new song.
  • Round 3 — Finals: the top 3 battle for the crown.
  • Vote totals reset each round — fresh tickets, fresh fundraising.

The Prizes

  • Grand Champion Month — the finals winner.
  • Best Solo and Best Group — top-scoring act of each division in the finals.
  • Trophies, bragging rights for the whole year, and glory for your birth month.
  • Every dollar raised supports The December Group's mission.

Voting: Where the Fundraising Happens

Applause is free — but votes are a dollar, and every vote counts.

Audience vote tickets

  1. Buy vote tickets at the door or during the show — $1 each, buy as many as you like.
  2. After each performance, a basket is passed through the audience.
  3. Drop tickets in the basket for the act that just performed — each ticket is a $1 vote for that month.
  4. Tickets are counted live on the tally board below. Highest totals win each category.
